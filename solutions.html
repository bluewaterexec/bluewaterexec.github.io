Why It Matters

Florida's permitting reform
changes everything

SB 1080

Automatic Refunds

Cities that exceed permit review timelines must issue automatic fee refunds. Manual tracking misses deadlines. PermitPace doesn't.

HB 803

Deemed Approved

Effective July 1, 2026 — permits not reviewed in time are automatically approved. That's massive risk for municipalities without real-time tracking.

6 Laws

Reform Wave

Florida's six-law permitting reform is the beginning. PermitPace is built as a compliance layer for this wave and the ones that follow.
