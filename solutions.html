Governance and Compliance

PermitPace

AI-powered permit compliance for government and developers.

PermitPace tracks municipal permitting workflows against Florida's landmark reform legislation, including SB 1080 automatic refunds and the HB 803 deemed-approved mandate effective July 1, 2026. Government staff and developers get real-time compliance status without navigating city portals.

  • SB 1080 refund tracking: automatic alerts before the refund window closes
  • HB 803 deemed-approved status: real-time threshold tracking as of July 1, 2026
  • Direct integration with existing permitting systems cities already use
  • Smart prioritization of permits most likely to breach compliance deadlines
